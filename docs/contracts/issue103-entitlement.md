# Which offers does a viewer's plan cover?

**Decision for #103 — READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION**, pending ChatGPT Web review of this architecture. At base `f9a4431ed4db85c29abd29a28ba4b90b82c32e9d`, the application can identify a service and several offer classes. To label a subscription offer “Included with your selected plan”, it also needs a viewer-declared plan and a current, reviewed assertion covering that plan, title, package and market. The label describes coverage under the declared selection. Account status and successful playback remain unverified. This phase delivers the contract and synthetic acceptance cases.

## Vocabulary and keys

* **Provider identity** is a canonical service or storefront ID. Resolve an external package by exact (source, market, package ID), then a reviewed one-to-one alias where applicable. Preserve both the source-reported package identity and canonical ID. Trim/case normalization as in #57 is sufficient for canonical slugs. Prohibit substring, prefix, fuzzy, brand/display-name and cross-package fallback in the entitlement path.
* **Subscription provider** is the service with which the viewer declares a holding. A standalone channel and a channel distributed through another provider have distinct package IDs. The host, channel and storefront can each have separate commercial roles.
* A **base plan**, **paid ad-supported tier** and **premium tier** are individually keyed plans offered by a subscription provider in a billing market. Each has a reviewed display name and descriptive class (`base | paid_ads | premium | other`). The class supplies UI labels only. Premium has no implicit inheritance. A paid plan with ads remains a subscription.
* A **channel add-on** is a separately selected package bound to an exact host, distribution channel and market. Multiple children can be held together. A standalone subscription with the same brand supplies no grant for the hosted channel.
* **Rental** and **purchase** are separate paid actions for a particular offer. Preserve each action, rendition and price/currency where supplied. A rent/buy account or price observation says nothing about an existing rental, purchase or subscription.
* **Free with ads** means explicit ad-funded access without a paid subscription for this title and market. Explicit free access without an ad-funding assertion keeps `offer_type=free` and a “Free” label. Advertising capability, a provider category and the name of a paid ads tier are insufficient.
* **Unspecified plan** means the viewer has not identified one. **Unsupported plan** means a declared plan has no supported mapping for the market/date. Keep these reasons distinct, including after a formerly supported product retires. Both yield unknown for plan-dependent offers. Public and transactional offers are evaluated independently.

## Inputs and evidence boundary

Evaluate at an injected UTC instant. The fact key comprises typed title (movie/series, parent series TMDb ID and explicit season/episode as applicable), source, market, exact source package, canonical provider, offer class and variant when its terms differ. Keep valid intervals half open, `[from, until)`. Missing freshness or uncertain identity cannot support a positive. A series result cannot silently qualify all episodes, and a season assertion cannot qualify a sibling season.

Each usable coverage assertion carries the fact key or an explicitly reviewed scope that contains it, the applicable plan IDs, allowed/denied effect, billing and viewing markets, effective window, observation time, freshness policy and a reviewable provenance reference. Store the original observation's source and package lineage with that reference. Expose a safe source label, scope, date and reason in public responses. Keep raw documents, account identifiers, signed URLs and private lookup keys out of fixtures and public payloads.

Accept a scoped affirmative or exclusion from a reviewed first-party title/plan rule, or from a reviewed first-party product rule whose explicit applicability includes this identified title/package. A product catalogue entry can establish that a plan is sold. A verified current title offer is a separate prerequisite. A catalogue listing, account sign-up, playback link, price, generic marketing copy, last check timestamp, or aggregator agreement supplies neither title-plan coverage nor playback evidence. Supported source fidelity is a prerequisite per fact. Enable a source/market mapping for positive results only after its fields and scope pass offline review. Until then return `UNKNOWN`.

Keep billing market, current location, offer market and VPN candidate regions distinct. Paid coverage applies in the billing market by default only when the offer rule matches there. A different market needs affirmative portability for that billing/viewing pair and the exact product. A documented prohibition yields `BLOCKED`. A missing portability rule yields `UNKNOWN`. Explicit worldwide title distribution still needs independently applicable plan terms for paid access. Absence of territory never implies worldwide. VPN affects potential reach only.

## Per-offer decision

Evaluate current, identity-bound evidence first. An unresolved package, material conflict for the *same* scope, stale evidence, unsupported class, or missing territory produces `UNKNOWN` with the corresponding reason. Keep conflicting observations inspectable. Country-specific disagreements and mixed transaction classes remain separate rows. For a paid plan check territorial constraints, declared base holding, supported plan, applicable title rule and then channel selection. Explicit applicable exclusions block. Among equally applicable allow/deny facts return `EVIDENCE_CONFLICT`; an out-of-window or differently scoped fact contributes neither. Missing observations never establish a denial.

| Verdict | Minimum facts after the evidence gate | User-facing meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `INCLUDED` | Held base and exact supported plan with affirmative title coverage. For an add-on, the rule must cover this channel offer with that base plan, and the viewer must also declare the exact child. Alternative public case requires explicit, unconditional free access for the title/market. | “Included with your selected plan”, “Included with your channel”, or “Free”, according to reason. |
| `REQUIRES_ADDON` | Current channel offer and scoped host relationship, eligible held base plan, affirmative rule for this combination, and viewer-confirmed missing child. | Name the channel and its host. A missing prerequisite base yields `BLOCKED / BASE_NOT_HELD` when explicitly unowned. An unanswered prerequisite yields `UNKNOWN`. |
| `RENT_OR_BUY` | Current, explicitly typed rental or purchase action for this market. Price may be absent. Preserve separate actions and terms. | “Rent” / “Buy”, optionally priced. Account selection may filter stores but never implies ownership. |
| `FREE_WITH_ADS` | Current, explicit title-level offer of free ad-funded access in this market, with applicable access conditions satisfied. | “Free with ads”. A required but unconfirmed signup/other condition yields unknown until its applicability is resolved. |
| `BLOCKED` | Affirmative applicable plan exclusion, region restriction, or explicitly unheld required base. | Explain which condition blocks this offer for the declared selection. Keep other offers visible. |
| `UNKNOWN` | Listing-only, unconfirmed holding, unsupported tier, unresolved mapping, stale/missing/ambiguous evidence, or conflicting applicable rules. | Explain the missing fact and show “Confirm your plan” only when user input can resolve it. |

## Services and wire contract

1. **Selection catalogue.** Extend `GET /api/providers/?billing_country=NL` additively with `plan_options[] {plan_id, display_name, class, billing_country, caveat}`, `channel_options[] {package_id, parent_provider, country, display_name, caveat}`, and the viewer's saved selection. Return only reviewed selectable options for that market, plus the state/label of an existing unsupported selection. Include an explicit “I don't know / my plan isn't listed” choice. UI explains that “With ads” and “Free with ads” describe different products.
2. **Selection write.** Add idempotent `PUT /api/subscriptions/{provider_slug}/markets/{billing_country}/` with `{holding: yes|no|unspecified, plan_state: selected|unspecified|unsupported, plan_id: string|null, channels: [{package_id, holding: yes|no}], revision: integer}`. Keys are exact. A selected plan must belong to the provider/market and requires `holding=yes`. Use null for an unlisted plan rather than accepting arbitrary new catalogue IDs. Return 400 on inconsistent input, duplicate children or wrong host/market. PUT replaces the channel declarations for that profile. Omitted children are unspecified. Atomically compare-and-increment the revision (zero for creation), return 409 on stale writes, and return 200 on an identical replay without changing the revision. Retain an explicitly selected channel when the base is marked unowned so the missing base remains explainable. Resolve the viewer on the server.
3. **Single backend resolver.** Extend `classify_offer` with an internal pure evaluation step taking (offer facts, applicable rules, viewer market selection, as-of). Return `entitlement: {verdict, reason, provider, offer_type, plan_id?, required_base?, required_channel?, offer_country, billing_country?, evidence_state, source_label?, observed_at?, caveats[]}`. Keep the safe source package label/ID and typed scope alongside the offer. Use stable reason codes in the [fixture corpus](issue103-entitlement-examples.json). Project this object on current, source-reported and historical rows within their existing partitions. Bulk-load market rules and holdings once per list request. Never use display text as a join key.
4. **Compatibility.** Preserve current response fields and raw offer classes. Derive `is_entitled` from a positive coverage result (`INCLUDED` or `FREE_WITH_ADS`), and `is_accessible` from that result plus current evidence, language and reach. A transactional row has `requires_payment=true` and can appear in `purchase_offers` independently of `is_entitled`. Preserve prices and all alternative offers. A missing v1 entitlement object on an older payload displays “Plan access unconfirmed” for a plan-dependent row. Update readers of legacy booleans/labels with the resolver change to avoid the current “included” string on unknown rows.
5. **Title projections and filters.** Among current, language-compatible, reachable offers, an accessible local positive wins, followed by a potential VPN positive. If there is no positive and coverage remains indeterminate on a potentially applicable offer, show `unknown` and preserve any known rent/buy options. Otherwise a reachable transactional action can yield `purchase_only`; known unmet requirements or offers only elsewhere yield `available_elsewhere`. A source report alone stays unconfirmed. Preserve existing empty/checking/last-known states, including `needs_recheck` for stale-only history. Provider/country/access-type discovery filters may show where an offer was seen. “Included in my plan” must match a qualifying subscription/channel row. “Watchable” must match an accessible row. When filters are combined, require **one row** satisfying all requested offer predicates so a local rental cannot borrow inclusion from a foreign subscription. Rank only after eligibility. Use the same projections for detail, cards, catalogue, watchlist/report, watched lists and “why can't I watch?”.
6. **Frontend.** Extend [API types](../../frontend/lib/api.ts) with the six-value union and structured selection/result. The subscriptions grid edits the market profile and offers a reason for unsupported plans, plus a host-labelled selector per channel. On conflict, reload the saved selection and keep the draft visible. The offer row renders the server's verdict/reason, country, plan/channel caveat and paid action; source-report and last-known sections retain their evidence labels. Recompute on profile/location changes, including client navigation/back caches. A selection save requires no source refresh.

## Deterministic fixture and acceptance contract

The [JSON corpus](issue103-entitlement-examples.json) is shared input for a future backend resolver/API harness and frontend response/UX harness. All names and rights are synthetic. Merge each case's `viewer` over `viewerDefaults` and each offer over `offerDefaults` independently, retaining explicit nulls. All rows share the fixture title ID. `scope=season:N` uses the parent series ID and season N. Use `asOf` in place of the system clock.

* The catalogue knows `aster` (plans `base`, `ads`, `premium` in each depicted market), its hosted `orbit_via_aster`, and the independent provider `aster_plus`. The viewer's holding and plan apply only to their provider. The channel field declares only the known child for that host. `orbit_unknown` has no reviewed package relationship. An absent distinct provider holding is unspecified.
* `allow:X` / `deny:X` supply one current first-party assertion for that offer key and plan X. `conflict:X` supplies both; `none` supplies neither. `ruleScope=same` copies typed scope; an explicit scope replaces it. `ruleUntil` is exclusive. `portability` describes evidence for the viewer billing/offer-market pair. All synthetic rules and verified offers are fresh at the frozen instant except where a case overrides the input. For explicit free/rent/buy classes, a verified row asserts that exact public/transactional action. A source-reported or stale row never satisfies the current evidence gate.
* `expect.rows[i]` gives the exact (verdict, primary reason) for input offer i before sorting. `expect.title` gives the aggregate watchability status. The input order labels rows solely for fixture assertions. Permuting offers must preserve every keyed result and the title state.

## Migration impact and delivery order

This design changes no runtime or database schema. The implementation should add market-scoped product definitions and a viewer market profile keyed by (viewer, provider, billing market), with child channel declarations unique per profile and package. Use stable product IDs and effective dates. Retain `Subscription` and its uniqueness rule for compatibility. Legacy rows seed a coarse holding only at read time; they supply no billing market, plan or confirmed channel absence. Avoid copying the viewer's current country into historical billing fields. Name-only legacy rows remain visible for confirmation. Explicit unsupported plans survive catalogue retirement without switching to the first available plan.

Make a separate source-attributed coverage table keyed by stable offer identity, source package, product, assertion scope and validity/revision. Preserve original observations independently when sources disagree. Do not change the stable identities of `Offer`, `OfferEvidence` or `OfferPrice`, or re-key variants to a plan. Follow the existing historical migration precautions around `tracker.0033` (in particular, adding relations traversed by the historical provider-deletion collector). Test from zero with the real migration graph and disposable PostgreSQL before choosing FK dependencies. Index the lookup by offer/market and the viewer lookup by profile. Verify no per-title query growth.

First introduce the pure evaluator and shared fixtures, then additive storage and a read adapter, then the selection API and UI, then all read/filter projections together. Preserve coarse-mode endpoints. A legacy removal must revoke the effective selected holdings for that provider across markets atomically; a legacy positive supplies no plan, never overwrites an explicit plan for the same holding, and cannot reactivate a revoked plan. Prefer read rollback with additive data retained. Run the migration and acceptance checks before enabling any product mapping. No production migration or collection is part of this handoff.
