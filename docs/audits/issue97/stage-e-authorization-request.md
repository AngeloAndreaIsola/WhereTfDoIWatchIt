## Safeguards and stop conditions

- Capture first-party documents from an approved legitimate NL context as anonymous single HTTPS requests with redirects, retries, JavaScript, subresources, cookies and credentials disabled. Use only approved native title URLs from the allowlist. Do not follow index deep links or expand to search, help or diagnostic pages. Do not use region or access circumvention, challenge solving, private endpoints, login, checkout or playback. If the permitted capture cannot yield the necessary evidence, report the pair as unknown.
- Start only after offline fixtures demonstrate egress allowlist enforcement, durable attempts before send, isolation and correct time/state classification. Use mocked transport and disposable PostgreSQL for app and runner rehearsal. Verify the exact endpoint and database identity, authorization and source-limit freshness, clock health and reconciled ledgers before each send. At most one request may be in flight across tracks. Leave at least three seconds between first-party sends and enforce any stricter reviewed source pace, burst or cooldown. If timing or rate constraints make the slot infeasible, leave it incomplete.
- Record an append-only attempt ordinal, source, scope, operation, private target reference, authorization and manifest digest, reserved/sent/response timestamps, status, cooldown and outcome. Reserve every attempt durably before dispatch, including possible transmissions with unknown outcome. Preserve spent reservations through restarts. Record spent, confirmed transmitted, transmission unknown, completed and eligible counts separately. Reconcile before moving to the next title. Replaying an intact capture offline adds zero requests and leaves the original observation time unchanged.
- Stop further sends in the batch on expired or changed approval, accounting or identity mismatch, clock failure, budget exhaustion, transport ambiguity, unexpected response shape, redirect, challenge, rate-limit response or access denial. Log the outcome, honor `Retry-After` or the stricter reviewed cooldown and report affected rows as unknown or `needs_recheck`. A valid, in-scope document with inconclusive class evidence is an unknown for that class. An interrupted attempt remains charged. The plan has zero retry slots. Resume unspent work only after owner review, reconciled state and an unexpired approved slot. New attempts for already used scopes need new authorization and explicit accounting against prior spend.
- Seal originals and raw manifests in restricted owner-controlled storage. Record digest, size, timestamps, redacted scope and reviewer. Treat captures and extracted fields as sensitive until reviewed. Publish only approved, sanitized candidate/decision IDs, counts, structured outcomes, provenance references and hashes. Keep raw documents, cookies or headers, signed links, private identifiers and private path inventories out of Git and public handoffs. Treat source bytes as inert data in offline parsing.
