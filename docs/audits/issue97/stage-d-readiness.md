## Proposed accounting and minimum reviewed changes

1. Use a separately reviewed, immutable Stage D manifest and policy for the direct bounded GraphQL path. Retain v1 as a separate fixed policy. Bind the new digest, stable experiment ID, disposable PostgreSQL cluster/database identity, stage and operation ceilings, retry policy, evidence reference or digest, and expiry into the authorization. A renewed HMAC for the same reviewed experiment continues its durable state. Record any new phase policy as an append-only revision of the same experiment, reconciled with prior spend and referring to completed checkpoints. Never reset the budget or rely on a new database to continue an existing approval. Admission rejects arbitrary CSV imports, title edits, unlisted seasons and the held row. A code review is required since the current manifest, commands and transport hard-code v1.
2. Read the runtime source receipt and the independent exact-scope permission, quota and rate-policy evidence before each attempt. The reviewed configuration must agree on the sole HTTPS endpoint, NL, fixed scope IDs, allowed documents/operations, validity window, reviewer/reference, allowable retries, pace and maximum requests. The default receipt uses `search` and `seasons`. Experiment evidence should name `GetSearchTitles` and, only when reviewed, the exact season document under `GetTitleNode`. Stage D must enforce both boundaries. Keep the existing one-hour evidence freshness rule. For metered usage, require a current balance for the same upstream window, reconciled across callers. A reviewed unmetered classification still needs a finite authorized ceiling and rate policy.
3. Reuse the durable experiment reservations and ordinary receipt accounting. Use the same source serialization, cooldown and receipt guard across paths wherever both can use the same grant. In the disposable target, dedicate egress and exclude other work on the grant unless all callers share one authoritative budget. Under the source and experiment locks, atomically reserve one unit against the durable experiment, receipt/quota, scope, scope-operation and global operation limits. Commit the reservation before the single transport send. Use a consistent lock order and keep the in-flight marker through the outcome commit. A refused reservation produces zero sends. An interrupted reservation spends one even if transmission cannot be confirmed. Never refund or reissue it automatically. No redirects, implicit HTTP retries, browser, or provider fallback.
4. Keep three counts. `notte_invocations` belongs to the historical or separate Notte run ledger, split by probe and collection. Polls of an existing run are management events. `http_reservations_spent` counts every committed reservation and is the enforcement counter. Report `http_transmitted_confirmed` only when a correlated upstream response or retained egress evidence confirms transmission. A transport handoff without either is `http_transmission_unknown`, charged against the same cap. A validated response also needs a separate observation checkpoint. Store attempt ordinal and retry predecessor, typed scope and phase, document/variables digest, method and destination, permission/manifest reference, reservation and response UTC times, status and retry/cooldown metadata, and response digest in append-only evidence. Keep credentials outside the logs. The present experiment schema has part of this metadata. Link the additional events to the existing attempt ID, with alerts when either receipt or experiment accounting disagrees.
5. Reuse the strict offline matching path for collection into isolated artifacts. A Stage D collector needs separate title and season completion states and the optional series operation. The current ordinary adapter can auto-fetch series seasons and fall back to title/year when a result has no ID. The experiment proxy also rejects season offers and filters expected offer class. Neither behavior supplies the required exact, title-first evidence contract. Keep identity decisions strict, retain unknown mapping/parse outcomes with raw evidence references, and retain source-reported offer variants separately from comparable service/class presence. Review the offer-variant contract against the pinned draft before classifying a Stage D output. [PR #198](https://github.com/AngeloAndreaIsola/WhereTfDoIWatchIt/pull/198)

These are planning ceilings. A title operation and a season operation each get one first-send slot. An unused or blocked slot cannot transfer to another operation, identity or source. The first recommended approval is for the 12 title calls, with seasons reviewed only after inspecting the title checkpoints. No HTTP attempts in this plan are funded by unused Notte invocations. If the historical proposal for 25 low-level attempts is intended to include the canary, its remaining balance cannot be established from the retained artifacts. If a separate future approval allocates 25 new Stage D attempts after admitting Coherence, 18 first sends would leave at most seven contingency attempts. Such a policy would still require a capped one-retry allowance per eligible operation and a separate review before enabling any retry. The default Stage D plan has no retry capacity.

Propose one in-flight request, a burst of one, and a gap of at least five seconds after the last response across titles, operations and resumes, subject to stricter reviewed upstream pacing. Any long wait ends the current checkpoint window if permission, quota evidence or HMAC would expire first. Existing v1 rate evidence requires `max_burst` at least equal to the source attempt ceiling even though the runner sends serially. An expanded policy cannot pass that unchanged gate with a genuine smaller provider burst. Preserve the gate pending review. If upstream permits a smaller burst, a separately reviewed change must enforce the actual burst and interval, with durable state across resumes, before accepting that rate evidence. Never supply the experiment ceiling as the provider burst just to satisfy validation.

## Checkpoints, stop conditions and recovery

1. **Before authorization.** Review the resolved identities, exact commands and payloads, per-operation ceilings, and the real evidence. Verify exact source and country rows and run an expanded read-only preflight on the intended disposable target. Check database identity and schema, unused or reconciled durable reservations, no overlapping queue or unfinished run, advisory locks, and exclusive grant ownership. Then obtain a separately signed authorization with the exact scope and expiry. This task stops before any authorization or runtime change.
2. **First reviewed batch.** Propose one movie and one series, The Godfather and Breaking Bad, as the first two of the 12 title searches. Pause for manual review of raw capture hashes, successful typed matches, offer market, response classification, and the accounting totals. Resume only from the same durable state. For the remaining ten searches, require a checkpoint after each request and operator review after each group of at most five. The optional phase starts only after title review and a signed policy explicitly authorizes seasons.
3. **Stop before any next send** on absent, expired or changed evidence, budget exhaustion, identity or market mismatch, malformed/unexpected response, unresolved identity, unexpected transport, lock/queue contention, or inconsistent ledger and artifact state. On HTTP 429, record the charged response and enforce the reviewed cooldown and `Retry-After` when present. On HTTP 401/403 or a redirect, stop with the response recorded and do not follow. A challenge or access refusal stops the run. A transport timeout, reset, uncertain send, or lost response/outcome record is charged and globally pauses the experiment for reconciliation. An exact title with no listed offers can be checkpointed as an unknown availability result without initiating additional calls.
4. **Resume idempotently.** Atomically save or verify a sanitized response artifact and its hash before marking `(experiment, typed scope, operation)` complete. Keep the observation timestamp and provenance through replays. Resume skips completed matching checkpoints. A response without a checkpoint may be replayed offline from intact, identity-checked evidence. A reservation without reliable evidence blocks automatic continuation and stays spent. Any subsequent authorized attempt must reference the prior ordinal and consume a fresh slot. If the operator cannot reconcile the attempt, retain it as unknown and keep the run blocked. Imported application rows and JustWatch-derived offers carry same-source evidence. Fresh independent comparison needs its own approved capture and time window.
